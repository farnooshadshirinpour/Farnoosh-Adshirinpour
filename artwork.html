Artwork

Painting and drawing is where my interest in space, light, and depth began. These are selected pieces in digital painting, oil, charcoal, and ink. Select any image to open it at full size.

Digital painting

Digital portraits, each working with a different light and colour setup: warm against cool, soft daylight, and a limited palette. The last two items are short clips of paintings coming together.

Biophilia

A series of oil paintings exploring the human relationship with nature through the philosophy of Biophilia. By integrating human and natural forms into a unified narrative, the project investigates the dissolution of the boundary between human and the natural world.

Rocks

A visual study inspired by the principles of Xieyi painting, investigating how close observation and visual interpretation can convey character beyond physical form.

Mountains

Challenged conventional perceptions of mountains through expressive color and composition, reinterpreting familiar landscapes as vibrant and dynamic environments.

The Battle Between Light and Darkness

Empty rooms where light enters through a window or a door. The dark room stands for fear and difficulty, and the light for hope. A chair or a cup suggests someone who has stayed in the shadow. As the series goes on, more light arrives, in the steam from a cup and the shadow of a plant.

Femme

A figurative project examining body diversity, aging, and female identity through observational drawing, emphasizing the individuality of the female body beyond idealized representation. Nude figure studies.

I am a visual artist with a BFA in Visual Arts (Painting). My practice spans traditional painting, digital art, and visual development. Over the years, I have explored different ways of working with images, from developing concepts and visual narratives to experimenting with digital tools and 3D environments.

I enjoy the process of learning new techniques, understanding the tools behind creative work, and finding new ways to bring ideas to life.

Alongside my artistic practice, I have developed an interest in programming, interactive design, and human-computer interaction, particularly in how technology can expand the possibilities of visual creation and digital experiences.
